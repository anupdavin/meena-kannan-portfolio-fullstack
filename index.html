5 Paradigms

Architectural breakdown of modern AI paradigms: goal decomposition loops, test-time compute, dense vector indexing, hybrid retrieval grounding, and host-tool communication standards.

Goal-driven autonomous agents executing continuous perception-action loops with memory, tools, and observation feedback (AutoGPT, Devin, LangGraph).

High-dimensional similarity search engine indexed with HNSW and IVFFlat over 1536d dense embeddings for sub-15ms nearest-neighbor retrieval (pgvector, Qdrant).

Enterprise external knowledge grounding combining hybrid BM25 lexical ranking and dense vector rerank for sub-180ms grounded responses with strict citations.

5. Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Open JSON-RPC 2.0 protocol over stdio and SSE connecting AI models to external tools, databases, and APIs without ambient credentials (Anthropic MCP standard).
